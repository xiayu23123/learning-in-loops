Publishing on your own site can seem old-fashioned. There is no algorithm promising an audience, no ready-made stream, and no number telling you whether the day was successful.

That is exactly why it matters.

On a personal website, an idea can take the shape it needs. A short note can stay short. A long argument does not have to become a thread. Old work remains findable without competing against the present moment.

## A durable address

The most valuable part is not the software. It is having an address that can outlast whichever platform is fashionable this year. The design can change. The hosting can move. The address remains yours.

The small web is not small because it lacks reach. It is small because each part has a human scale.
