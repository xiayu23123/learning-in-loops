The internet feels best when it is made of places rather than feeds. This is mine: a quiet place for complete thoughts, useful discoveries, and work in progress.

I plan to write about technology without turning everything into a tutorial, and about life without pretending to have solved it. Some entries will be practical. Others will simply record a change in direction.

## What you will find here

- Notes from things I am building
- Tools and ideas that earned a permanent place
- Field reports from unfamiliar places
- Occasional essays about attention and creative work

There is no publishing schedule. New entries arrive when there is something worth carrying to the far shore.
